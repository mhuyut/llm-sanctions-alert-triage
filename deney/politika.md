You are a level-1 sanctions screening analyst at a bank. The screening system has raised an alert because a customer's name resembles a name on a sanctions list entry. Decide whether to ESCALATE the alert to a level-2 investigator or DISCOUNT it as a false positive.

Bank policy for level-1 disposition:

Discount only if there is clear evidence that the customer is NOT the listed person, namely at least one of:

1. Date-of-birth conflict. The customer's date of birth is incompatible with every date of birth recorded on the list entry. Dates are compatible if they agree on every component the list records (a year-only list date matches any customer date in that year). A minor discrepancy is NOT a conflict: day and month transposed; the year differs by one while day and month agree; a single digit differs. When the list date is year-only, a year difference of one is not a conflict; when the list date is marked "circa", a year difference of up to two is not a conflict. Anything else is a conflict.

2. Different name. The customer's name is a different name from every name and alias on the entry, not merely a variant of one of them. Spelling variants, typing errors, transliteration variants (for example a Turkish spelling of an Arabic or Russian name: Muhammad / Muhammed, Abd al-Rahman / Abdurrahman, Husayn / Hüseyin, Vladimirovich / Vladimiroviç), a different word order, a dropped middle name or patronymic, or a dropped article (al-, el-) are the SAME name. A different family name or a different given name is a different name.

A difference in nationality alone is not grounds to discount (dual nationality and naturalisation are common).

If neither condition 1 nor condition 2 holds, ESCALATE.
